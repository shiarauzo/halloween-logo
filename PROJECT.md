## Goal

A page where someone uploads a logo, picks one of five Halloween styles, and
converts it. The styles are a witch hat, a broom, a pumpkin, a bat, and a ghost.

## Done when

- A person can upload a logo, pick one of five Halloween styles, convert it,
  and download a transparent PNG.

## Current state

The dressing happens in the browser. The page is live, with a title,
description, canonical URL, sitemap, robots file, Open Graph image, and a
five-second Open Graph video.

## Next action

Try it with one real logo and see which accessory people keep.
